# Channel Estimation and Equalization for MIMO Systems

**Period**: 2017

**Role**: author

## What was done

Statistical signal processing project: a matrix-based scheme for channel estimation and equalization in MIMO[^1] systems. The [full PDF](https://drive.proton.me/urls/X17ZP29GR4#zwkFN_Cx9ipD) documents the scheme and the results.

[^1]: *MIMO*, Multiple Input, Multiple Output - a transmission system with multiple antennas at both the transmitter and the receiver.
