# Nonlinear devices in guitar speakers

**Period**: 01/2021 - 05/2021

**Role**: support and literature review, not the author of the exam

**Context**: project for the UNIVPM university course [Multirate DSP and Adaptive Filterbanks](https://guide.univpm.it/af.php?af=113475) (academic year 2020/2021).

## What was done

The project analyzes nonlinear devices (distortion, overdrive) applied to guitar speakers, with MATLAB/C++ algorithms built on Volterra series and exponential sweep techniques for system characterization. The final research and the exam report were carried out by a fellow student, who took the exam; the contribution documented here was supportive, in the literature review and in helping with the implementation, not the independent running of the project.
