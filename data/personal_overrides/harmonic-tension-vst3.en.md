VST3 plugin in C++ on JUCE that analyzes incoming MIDI in real time and turns harmony into a control signal for stage lights rather than for sound. It implements Elaine Chew's Spiral Array Model, a geometric representation of tonal harmony: from the played notes and their duration it computes harmonic tension, cloud diameter and tensile strain, and estimates the current key as the point of the spiral closest to the notes' center of effect.

The system was tested live at the Festivalle electronic music festival, in the Valle dei Templi, in August 2019, with tension mapped to the brightness and patterns of the lights ([demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wB-U9s4ASQo)). The [document on the model](https://drive.proton.me/urls/KHMJ76J6RR#cSM_oJpX6Ky4) describes the geometry and the use case in more detail.
