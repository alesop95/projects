A local MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that turns Claude Desktop into a research assistant for Italian law. The reasoning happens in Claude Desktop on the existing subscription, with no pay-per-use API, and the server exposes three tools: BM25 full-text search over SQLite FTS5 with citable excerpts, retrieval of an article's full text by URN, and corpus information. No GPU or embeddings are needed. A one-click Windows installer sets up git and uv, builds the index, registers the server in Claude Desktop and schedules the daily data refresh.

The corpus starts from a local clone of the italia-corpus project, supplemented with the civil code, the penal code and the code of civil procedure downloaded from Normattiva. An audit in July 2026 measured that the clone lacked 9,313 of the 13,730 ordinary laws not repealed, 1,584 of the 1,636 decree-laws and the Constitution, because it mirrors only Normattiva's predefined collections. The gaps are now filled from Normattiva's official Open Data API, through a bulk export in Akoma Ntoso spread over several days, and a completeness check reports the acts still missing. At the end of July the penal code was brought up to date after constitutional court ruling 108/2026.

In August a subject audit on residential property purchases checked seventeen acts: of the three gaps found, two were filled, while the third, law 448/1998, is not returned by the source and remains declared as missing. Integrating case law has been assessed in a feasibility study and is not implemented. It is an informational tool, not legal advice, and lexical search cannot guarantee that an article is in force without checking Normattiva.
