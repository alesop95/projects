Civic participation web platform for young people in Civitanova Marche: events with RSVP, forum, proposals with voting and an approval queue, civic quizzes, polls, a profile with a digital membership card, a city map, civic spaces, a timeline, press review, mentorship, gallery and document archive, webinars, push notifications and an admin panel with statistics and member management. It started from a high-fidelity design prototype, kept as a reference, and is rebuilt in Next.js 16 with Prisma 7 on PostgreSQL and Auth.js (credentials and Google).

Status: the planned features are implemented and mostly verified in the browser, including the protection measures (rate limiting, input validation, moderation, GDPR account deletion). The suite counts 353 tests (Vitest for the logic, Playwright end-to-end against a real Postgres), and CI also boots the app on the Cloudflare Workers runtime. The application is not in production yet: the choice of hosting platform is on hold and the external services (storage, email, notifications) still have to be configured.
