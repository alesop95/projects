Experimental work of a Bachelor's thesis in Electronic Engineering: it measures the clock drift of two embedded platforms using GPS time as the reference. An Arduino Uno, which keeps time with the software counter millis(), and an STM32 Nucleo of the L1 family, which uses its own hardware RTC with an external 32.768 kHz crystal, receive NMEA sentences from the same GPS module and log the offset over several hours.

On the Arduino a sketch reads the time from the $GPGGA sentence and sends it with millis() to a Python logger that writes CSV. The STM32 firmware, on Contiki OS and HAL, configures the RTC prescalers for a 1 Hz tick from the LSE oscillator and compares it periodically with GPS. Over about four hours the Arduino drifts by about 5 seconds and the STM32 by about 1.6: the crystal RTC is about three times more stable.
