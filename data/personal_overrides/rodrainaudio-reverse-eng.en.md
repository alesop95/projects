A LaTeX technical report on the hardware reverse engineering of a cheap "Rod Rain audio" headphone amplifier, a rebrand with an embedded USB DAC module. The chassis follows the general topology of the Beyerdynamic A1 headphone amplifier, while the DAC path combines a SaviTech SA9023 USB-to-I2S receiver with an ESS ES9023 DAC chip, reconstructed from datasheets, visual inspection of the board and behavior observed under Windows 11.

The document separates what is observed or documented from what remains a hypothesis, and one section corrects four wrong assumptions from an earlier draft, among them treating the ES9023 as a USB device instead of an I2S-input DAC fed by a separate USB receiver, and a wrong estimate of the amplifier's output impedance and gain. It ends with measurement procedures for the questions still open and a parametric SPICE model.
