Flutter mobile app for programming cross-training workouts, built from specifications and datasets derived from an existing PowerPoint deck and Excel workbook. Workouts combine exercises from three families, weightlifting, gymnastics and locomotion, into non-repeating groups of one, two or three elements across a cycle. The logic comes from an Excel prototype that drew combinations without replacement with RANDBETWEEN over a shrinking range, a Fisher-Yates variant, and is rewritten as a pure Dart class with filters for equipment, level and enabled groups, covered by tests.

Status: on hold. Specifications, JSON datasets, five visual directions with mockups and a scaffold with the main screens and a Tabata timer are written but not compiled, because Flutter was not installed on the development machine.
