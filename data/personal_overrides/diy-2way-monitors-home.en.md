Design and build of a pair of two-way studio monitors for a home listening position under a sloped ceiling that cannot be acoustically treated. The project starts from the room: the listening position is measured, a geometric model of the room is validated against the measurement, the loudspeaker is designed, and only at the end is the placement optimized with both models together. The target is a response within plus or minus 2 dB from 60 Hz to about 20 kHz, on axis and up to ten degrees off axis, measured at the real listening position.

The workflow runs on Ubuntu Studio: REW, Blender, Octave with MATAA and FreeCAD run natively; the Windows programs with no alternative run under Wine, namely Akabak 3 for electroacoustic and room simulation, VACS, VituixCAD for crossover and directivity, and EASE Focus.

Status: the working environment is set up and documented step by step, with the four Windows programs working, the audio chain verified and a log of each intervention with its outcome. The physical part has not started: no measurements, no drivers bought, no cabinet. The current step is the geometric survey of the room.
